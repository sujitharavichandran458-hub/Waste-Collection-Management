ECOTRACK – RISK MANAGEMENT

Project Title:
Waste Collection Management and Environmental Data Analysis System

1. INTRODUCTION
Risk management identifies possible problems that may affect project development, performance and completion.

2. IDENTIFIED PROJECT RISKS

Risk 1: Database Connection Failure
Description: The application may fail to connect to PostgreSQL.
Impact: High
Mitigation: Verify database credentials, connection URL and PostgreSQL service.

Risk 2: Application Errors
Description: Errors may occur while adding, updating or retrieving records.
Impact: High
Mitigation: Perform functional testing and validate user inputs.

Risk 3: Data Loss
Description: Important project records may be lost.
Impact: High
Mitigation: Maintain regular database backups.

Risk 4: Data Analysis Errors
Description: Python scripts may produce incorrect results due to invalid or missing data.
Impact: Medium
Mitigation: Validate input data and verify analysis calculations.

Risk 5: Diagram and Documentation Errors
Description: Project diagrams or documents may be incomplete.
Impact: Medium
Mitigation: Review all diagrams and documents before submission.

Risk 6: Time Constraints
Description: Limited time may delay project completion.
Impact: High
Mitigation: Follow the project schedule and prioritize essential deliverables.

3. RISK MANAGEMENT STRATEGIES
- Identify risks during every development phase.
- Analyze the probability and impact of each risk.
- Apply preventive measures.
- Test the application regularly.
- Maintain backup copies of important files.
- Review project progress frequently.

4. CONCLUSION
Potential project risks are identified and suitable mitigation strategies are planned to ensure successful development and submission of the EcoTrack system.
